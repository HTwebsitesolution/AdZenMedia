# Launch plan (manual outreach only)

## Weeks 1–2: foundations
- Approve copy, privacy draft, contact details and at least one case study.
- Connect domain, switch email to live, delete sample leads.

## Channel 1: warm introductions (weeks 1–4)
- List 30 people who know field-service owners in the US or Canada (past clients, suppliers, peers).
- Ask each for one introduction, using a short forwardable note and the sector page link with `?utm_source=intro`.

## Channel 2: targeted sector outreach (weeks 2–8)
- Build a list of 50 inspection, testing and calibration firms (25 US, 25 Canada) from association directories.
- Send 10 personal messages a week, by hand. Link to the systems check with `?utm_source=outreach&utm_campaign=fs-us` or `fs-ca`.

## Channel 3: organic search (ongoing)
- One article every two weeks answering a buyer question, for example "How calibration labs can stop chasing certificate requests". Link each article to the sector page and the Quote Wizard.
- Add country pages only after 5+ inquiries from that country and one relevant case study.
